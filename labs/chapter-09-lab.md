# Chapter 9 Lab: Security Concepts, AAA, and Device Hardening

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Packet Tracer: harden a router by disabling Telnet, enabling SSH-only vty access, setting an enable secret, and configuring local AAA authentication; verify with show running-config and a failed Telnet attempt

## Key takeaway

Device hardening (SSH-only access, enable secret, AAA) and knowing TACACS+ vs RADIUS differences are core exam security skills.
